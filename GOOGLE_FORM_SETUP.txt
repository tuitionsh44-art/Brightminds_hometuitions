BRIGHTMINDS TUTOR GOOGLE FORM

The website is ready. To make “Start Teaching” open your real Google Form:
1. Create a Google Form using the questions shown on tutor-application.html.
2. Copy the Share link.
3. Open script.js.
4. Replace the empty value in TUTOR_GOOGLE_FORM_URL with your Google Form URL.
5. Save and republish/upload the website.

Example:
const TUTOR_GOOGLE_FORM_URL = "https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/YOUR_FORM_ID/viewform";
